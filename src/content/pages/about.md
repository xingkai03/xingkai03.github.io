Hi, I am Xingkai Fang. You can call me Xingkai or Kai.

I am a master's student in Medical Engineering at KTH Royal Institute of Technology in Sweden. Before that, I studied Biomedical Engineering at Shanghai Jiao Tong University in China.

My main interest is medical imaging: building better tools that can make diagnosis and treatment clearer, faster, and more useful for real clinical work.

I speak Chinese, English, German, and a little Swedish. This blog is where I share notes, projects, and things I am learning along the way.
